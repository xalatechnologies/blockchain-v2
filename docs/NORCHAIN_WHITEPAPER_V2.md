## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 The Problem

Traditional blockchain networks face critical challenges:
- **Regulatory uncertainty** preventing institutional adoption
- **Lack of compliance** with financial regulations (MiCA, VASP, AML/KYC)
- **Environmental concerns** from energy-intensive consensus
- **Absence of ethical finance** frameworks
- **Poor user experience** preventing mainstream adoption

### 1.2 The NorChain Solution

NorChain addresses these challenges through:
- **Built-in Compliance**: GDPR, NSM, MiCA, VASP-ready from genesis
- **Shariah Compliance**: Interest-free, profit-sharing, transparent charity
- **Energy Efficient**: PoSA consensus (Proof of Staked Authority)
- **Superior UX**: NEX Exchange (Firi-style interface on-chain)
- **AI Governance**: Smart AML, fraud detection, risk scoring

## 6. Tokenomics & Economics

### 6.1 NOR Token Utility

1. **Gas Fees**: All transactions require NOR
2. **Staking**: Lock NOR to earn rewards (8-12% APY)
3. **Governance**: Vote on proposals (1 NOR = 1 vote)
4. **Liquidity Mining**: Provide liquidity, earn NOR rewards
5. **Charity Contribution**: 2% of block rewards → Zakat pool

### 6.2 Deflationary Mechanisms

**Burn Mechanisms**:
- 0.25% of every transaction burned
- 50% of DEX fees burned
- Monthly validator burn auction
- **Target**: 50% supply reduction over 10 years

### 6.3 Staking Economics

**Validator Staking**:
- Minimum: 1M NOR
- Annual Yield: 6-8%
- Slashing: 10% for downtime, 50% for double-sign

**Delegator Staking**:
- Minimum: 100 NOR
- Annual Yield: 8-12% (varies by validator)
- No lock-up period
- Unstaking delay: 7 days
