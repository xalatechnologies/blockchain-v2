## LOGO SPECIFICATIONS FOR CMC

### Required Format:
- **Format**: PNG (with transparent background)
- **Size**: 200x200px minimum (512x512px recommended)
- **File size**: Under 1MB
- **Background**: Transparent (no white box)
- **Shape**: Square aspect ratio
- **Quality**: High resolution, clear, professional

### What CMC Looks For:
✅ Clear and recognizable
✅ Professional appearance
✅ Appropriate for crypto/blockchain
✅ Not copyrighted/stolen from other projects
✅ Matches project branding

❌ Avoid:
- Blurry or pixelated images
- Generic stock photos
- Copyrighted images
- Inappropriate content
- White backgrounds (use transparent)
