## 36 Public Trading & Launch Plan
Nor Chain enters the market through a **dual-launch path**:  
1️⃣ native DEX (NorSwap) for instant liquidity;  
2️⃣ centralized exchange (CEX) listings for mass visibility.

### Launch Timeline (Indicative)
| Week | Milestone |
| -- | -- |
| 0 | Mainnet go-live + DEX pairs (NOR/USDT, Dirhamat/USDT) |
| 2 | Bridge activation to BSC & Polygon |
| 4 | $800 000 LP lock announcement + audit release |
| 6 | Gate.io or BitMart listing application |
| 8 | CMC & CoinGecko listing approval |
| 10 | Trust Wallet & MetaMask Chainlist integration |
| 12 | Press & community roadshow (MENA + Kenya) |

### Exchange Listing Checklist
- ✅ Audited contracts & supply proof  
- ✅ Explorer (API + circulating supply)  
- ✅ Legal opinions (UAE, Kenya, EU)  
- ✅ Market maker agreement  
- ✅ Liquidity provision wallets  
- ✅ Brand assets for CMC/CG  

### Wallet Integration
- Register chain metadata on [chainlist.org](https://chainlist.org).  
- Submit pull request to Trust Wallet assets repo.  
- Request MetaMask featured network inclusion.  
- Integrate WalletConnect for mobile apps.
