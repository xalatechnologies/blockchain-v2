## 1. Executive Overview
**Nor Funds** transform Islamic finance into a borderless, transparent, and Shariah-compliant ecosystem.  
They allow banks, fintechs, and institutions to tokenize **real-world assets (RWA)**, automate compliance, and offer halal investment products backed by **gold, real estate, sukuk, SMEs, and ESG projects**.

> “Nor connects capital with conscience — turning finance into impact.”

## 3. Key Features
- **FundUnit Token Standard:** tokenized investor units with built-in NAV and jurisdictional controls.  
- **AI NAV Agent:** continuous valuation and risk monitoring.  
- **Shariah Oracle:** validates assets and fatwas on-chain.  
- **Zakat Engine:** automates 2.5 % purification and sends proceeds to verified charities.  
- **Compliance Core (XCC):** AML, KYC, GDPR and AAOIFI integrated by design.  
- **Bridge & DEX:** instant cross-chain liquidity for halal assets.
