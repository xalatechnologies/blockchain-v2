## Governance Evolution

### Phase 1 (2024-2025): Foundation Council
- 5 founding members (multisig 3-of-5)
- Emergency powers for network stability
- Community advisory votes

### Phase 2 (2025-2026): Hybrid Governance
- Council DAO (institutions)
- Validator DAO (node operators)
- Community DAO (token holders)
- Proposals require 2-of-3 DAO approval

### Phase 3 (2027+): Full Decentralization
- Community-only governance
- On-chain parameter changes
- No emergency overrides
- AI advisory layer (non-voting)
