## 💰 XHN Standalone Value Propositions

### 1. **Revenue Sharing** 🏦

**How it works:**
- XHN holders receive 60% of ALL protocol revenues
- Revenue sources: DEX fees, bridge fees, NFT marketplace, launchpad
- Distributed proportionally to staked XHN

**Example:**
```
Monthly Protocol Revenue: $100,000
├─ 60% to Stakers: $60,000
├─ 30% for Buyback & Burn: $30,000
└─ 10% to Treasury: $10,000

If you hold 10% of staked XHN:
Your monthly revenue share: $6,000
Your yearly revenue share: $72,000
```

**Multi-tier staking with bonus multipliers:**

| Lock Period | Base APY | Multiplier | Effective APY |
|-------------|----------|------------|---------------|
| 30 days | 30% | 1.0x | **30%** |
| 90 days | 30% | 1.5x | **45%** |
| 180 days | 30% | 2.0x | **60%** |
| 365 days | 30% | 3.0x | **90%** |

**Plus revenue share!**

**Example: 100,000 XHN staked for 1 year**
```

Total Annual Return: 100,000-140,000 XHN
ROI: 100-140%
```

### 3. **Buyback & Burn** 🔥

**Deflationary mechanism:**
- 30% of protocol revenue used to buy XHN from market
- Purchased XHN is permanently burned
- Reduces circulating supply → Increases value per token

**Impact over time:**
```
Year 1: 1,000,000,000 XHN max supply
        - 10,000,000 burned
        = 990,000,000 remaining

Year 5: 990,000,000 XHN
        - 200,000,000 burned (cumulative)
        = 790,000,000 remaining (21% reduction!)

Your XHN becomes scarcer and more valuable.
```

**Voting power:**
