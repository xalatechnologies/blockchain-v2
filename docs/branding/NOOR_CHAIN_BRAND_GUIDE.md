## Visual Identity

### Color Palette

#### Primary Colors

**Pure White** (Background, Clarity)
- HEX: `#FFFFFF`
- RGB: `255, 255, 255`
- Use: Backgrounds, cards, clean spaces

**Radiant Gold** (Primary Brand)
- HEX: `#FFD700`
- RGB: `255, 215, 0`
- Use: Primary CTAs, highlights, logo accents

**Deep Sapphire** (Trust, Depth)
- HEX: `#0F52BA`
- RGB: `15, 82, 186`
- Use: Text, buttons, headers

#### Secondary Colors

**Soft Moonlight** (Subtle backgrounds)
- HEX: `#F8F9FA`
- RGB: `248, 249, 250`

**Sunset Orange** (Accents, warnings)
- HEX: `#FF8C42`
- RGB: `255, 140, 66`

**Emerald Green** (Success, growth)
- HEX: `#50C878`
- RGB: `80, 200, 120`

**Midnight Navy** (Dark mode primary)
- HEX: `#1A1F36`
- RGB: `26, 31, 54`

### Typography

#### Primary Font: **Inter**
- **Headings**: Inter Bold/Black
- **Body**: Inter Regular/Medium
- **Code**: JetBrains Mono
- **Use**: Web, documentation, UI

#### Secondary Font: **Orbitron**
- **Use**: Futuristic headers, blockchain-specific content
- **Weights**: Medium, Bold

#### Arabic Support: **Noto Sans Arabic**
- **Use**: Arabic language content
- **Weights**: Regular, Bold

### Logo Concept

**Symbol**: Radiant Circle with Light Rays
- Geometric design combining:
  - Central luminous circle (representing Nor/Light)
  - Radiating light beams (6-8 rays)
  - Optional: Subtle crescent integration
  - Blockchain lattice pattern within rays

**Wordmark**: "NOOR CHAIN"
- Font: Orbitron Bold or custom geometric
- Letter spacing: Slightly expanded
- Case: ALL CAPS or Title Case
- Color: Gradient from Gold to Sapphire

**Icon-only**: Stylized "N" formed by intersecting light rays

### Logo Usage

✅ **Correct Usage**:
- Maintain minimum clear space (equal to height of "N")
- Use official color versions only
- Scale proportionally
- Use on contrasting backgrounds

❌ **Incorrect Usage**:
- Don't rotate or skew
- Don't change colors arbitrarily
- Don't add effects (shadows, outlines)
- Don't place on busy backgrounds without container

### UI Design Principles

1. **Light-First Design**
   - White/light backgrounds as default
   - Dark mode with Midnight Navy + Gold accents
   - Generous whitespace for clarity

2. **Glow Effects**
   - Subtle gold glow on hover states
   - Radial gradients for emphasis
   - Box shadows with soft gold tint

3. **Geometric Patterns**
   - Hexagonal grids (blockchain motif)
   - Circular elements (representing light)
   - Clean lines and sharp angles

4. **Animation**
   - Smooth fade transitions
   - Pulsing glow effects
   - Light ray animations on load

## Brand Evolution Notes

### From Nor to Nor

**Reason for Rebrand**:
> "Nor Chain evolved into Nor Chain to better represent our mission of bringing light, clarity, and ethical innovation to blockchain technology. The name 'Nor' embodies our commitment to transparency and guidance."

**What Changed**:
- Name: Nor Chain → Nor Chain
- Token: NOR → NOR
- Brand identity: New visual language
- Domain: xaheen.org → norchain.org

**What Stayed the Same**:
- Chain ID: 65001
- Technical architecture
- Validator addresses
- Core mission and values
- Community and governance

### Migration Message

> "Welcome to Nor Chain! Previously known as Nor Chain, we've evolved our brand to better reflect our mission of illuminating the blockchain space with transparency, trust, and ethical innovation. All technical parameters remain unchanged - same Chain ID (65001), same validators, same security. Only our name shines brighter."

## Brand Guidelines Summary

### DO ✅

- Use "Nor Chain" (two words, title case)
- Use "NOR" or "$NOR" for token ticker
- Maintain gold + sapphire color scheme
- Use light/illumination metaphors appropriately
- Emphasize transparency and compliance
- Include 🌙 emoji in social media when appropriate

### DON'T ❌

- Spell as "NorChain" (one word) in formal text
- Use old "Nor" or "NOR" branding
- Change official colors
- Use religious imagery excessively
- Overuse light metaphors (keep it balanced)
- Claim to be a "cryptocurrency" (we're a blockchain platform)
