## 🔒 Key Investor Protections

### **How We Keep Their Money Safe:**

1. **Multi-Sig Treasury (3-of-5)**
   - Investor is one of the 5 signers
   - Requires 3 signatures for spending
   - All transactions public on-chain

2. **Milestone-Based Release**
   - $150k immediate (legal/infrastructure)
   - $200k Month 3 (LOCKED until milestones hit)
   - $150k Month 6 (LOCKED until milestones hit)
   - Money returned if targets not met

3. **Liquidation Preference**
   - Investors paid 100% FIRST if company exits
   - Founders get $0 until investors made whole

4. **Founder Lockup**
   - 4-year vesting on equity
   - 4-year vesting on tokens
   - Enforced by smart contract
   - Can't dump and run

5. **Insurance & Audits**
   - $1.5M coverage (D&O, E&O, cyber)
   - Smart contract audits (CertiK + Quantstamp)
   - Financial audits (quarterly CPA)

6. **Board Oversight**
   - Lead investor gets board seat
   - Veto rights on major decisions
   - Monthly updates
   - Quarterly meetings

7. **Downside Protection**
   - Soft failure: Extended timeline
   - Hard failure: Funds returned
   - Liquidation: Investors paid first
